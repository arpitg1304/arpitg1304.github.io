Current focus

Agentic robotics

Robots as agents, not just policies. A reasoning model plans, calls skills and tools (perception, navigation, manipulation primitives, ROS services), checks its own progress and recovers when things go wrong. Where do agent loops make robots more capable, and where do they just add latency?

  1. goal“put the red cup in the sink”
  2. planfind cup → grasp → place in sink
  3. calldetect(“red cup”)✓
  4. callgrasp(cup_1)✗ slipped
  5. verifyre-plan grasp from new view✓
  6. callplace(cup_1, sink)✓

Agentic robotics · coming soon

The agent harness for the physical world.

A coding agent learns your robot arm, writes the program, rehearses it on a virtual copy of your rig, and runs it on the real arm only after you approve.

  • Learns the rig first. Finds the hardware, checks the cameras and records a versioned rig model.
  • Rehearses every run. A virtual copy runs first, shown as a ghost arm over the real one.
  • Safety in the runtime. Joint and speed limits, a table floor and an e-stop hold under any code. The model never sits in the control loop.
  • First alpha. Onboards a LeRobot SO-101 from a one-line brief in about two minutes.
